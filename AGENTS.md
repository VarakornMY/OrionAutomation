# Orion website requirements

- Use the supplied company artwork at `public/brand/orion-logo.jpg`. Do not
  replace it with an invented icon, monogram, or an AI-redrawn logo.
- Brand palette: background `#c8c1af`, highlight `#f1daac`, deep red `#9f0606`.
  Derived warm surfaces and dark text must retain accessible contrast.
- Do not create, restore, advertise, or publish the removed Sumi-Ka, Hotzilla,
  Benjy Cafe, or other unapproved restaurant demo portfolio projects. Their
  pages, routes, static files, images, and portfolio links were deliberately
  removed at the user's request. Do not fabricate replacement portfolio work.
  `npm run build` includes a regression guard against reintroducing these files
  or references in production source.
- Keep hosting static and free. The Python/admin/chatbot backend is deferred;
  do not enable Containers, paid APIs, or subscriptions without user approval.
- Keep the interface plain and direct: no promotional eyebrow labels,
  repetitive subtitles, decorative emoji or arrow glyphs, fake dashboards,
  or fabricated statistics. Prefer clear headings and useful content.
  Motion should respond to interaction and honour reduced-motion settings.
- Publish only confirmed company identity and contact details. Never invent a
  registration number, address, accreditation, client testimonial, partnership,
  or verification status. Keep business descriptions and policies consistent
  with the live website and actual services.
